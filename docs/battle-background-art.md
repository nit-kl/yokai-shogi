# Battle shrine backgrounds

Created with the built-in imagegen tool, 2026-09-05. No external stock art.
Runtime files: `client/public/assets/ui/battle-shrine.webp` (1536×1024) and
`client/public/assets/ui/battle-shrine-mobile.webp` (768×1152).
Convert original PNGs with `node scripts/prepare-battle-backgrounds.mjs <landscape.png> <portrait.png>`.
The picture element selects only the portrait resource on phones in portrait orientation.

## Landscape prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: finished landscape background illustration for a Japanese yokai shogi battle game, 1536x1024 landscape.
Primary request: a lavish supernatural shrine courtyard at midnight, premium Japanese fantasy game environment painting. Monumental vermilion torii gates with elaborate aged gold fittings frame the LEFT AND RIGHT margins, layers of ornate shrine roofs and sacred ropes fade into indigo mountain mist. A luminous detailed ivory full moon in the upper left sky, warm gold lanterns along stone steps, ethereal blue foxfire at the lower corners, delicate floating gold embers. Black lacquer, carved gilded wood, rain-polished stone reflecting lanterns, rich atmospheric depth. A subtle golden ritual circle engraved in the foreground stone.
Composition: wide symmetrical establishing shot, central 45 percent is quiet dark indigo mist and open courtyard because an opaque vertical board overlays the center. Put the luxurious architecture and lighting on the outer thirds, architectural eaves across top corners and luminous details near bottom corners so a center crop for mobile still has atmospheric edges. The entire scene must feel richly painted and immersive, not flat vector graphics.
Lighting: cinematic moonlight, restrained luminous gold, cobalt and violet shadows, luxurious and mysterious; visible rich details without excessive darkness.
Constraints: environment only, no people, no creatures, no characters, no game board, no grid, no UI, no lettering, no readable text, no logo, no watermark. Full bleed illustration.
