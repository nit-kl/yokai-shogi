Hyakkiban Privacy Policy

Operator: nit / Last updated: August 18, 2026

This English version is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version controls.

Independent developer nit (the “Operator”) establishes this Policy for information handled by the browser game Hyakkiban (the “Service”). The Service is designed to minimize collection of personal information and does not collect email addresses, legal names, phone numbers, or payment details.

  • The Service has no chat feature, except for preset expressions.

2. Purposes of Use

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving the Service, including saving and syncing progress;
  • Detecting and preventing cheating, multiple-account abuse, automation, and reward fraud;
  • Incident response and user support;
  • Aggregated usage statistics that do not identify individuals; and
  • Delivering and measuring rewarded ads and preventing ad fraud when a user chooses to watch.

3. Disclosure to Third Parties

The Operator does not provide collected information to third parties except as required by law or as follows:

  • The Service uses Cloudflare for delivery, compute, databases, and bot prevention. Cloudflare is treated as a service provider or equivalent party.
  • If you watch a rewarded ad, device and connection information required to deliver it is sent to an ad network, such as Google's advertising services, under that network's policies.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

The Service uses browser storage and similar technology to maintain login state and progress, including authentication tokens, local saves, and ad-consent records. Ad networks may use cookies or similar technology when rewarded ads are used.

6. Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of your account and data through support. The account is then suspended and physically deleted after 90 days.
  • For other access, correction, or deletion requests, contact support. Authentication identifiers may be requested to verify your identity.

7. Minors

The Service has no paid purchases and therefore no high-value payment risk. Rewarded ads are optional and subject to a daily limit. Display names are filtered for inappropriate expressions.

8. Use Outside Japan

The Service is operated from Japan and intended primarily for users in Japan. Japanese is the canonical language; the English UI is provided for convenience. This English translation is provided to make the Service more accessible. The Service is governed by Japanese law.

9. Changes

This Policy may be revised without prior notice. Important changes will be announced in the game or by similar means.

10. Contact

For questions about this Policy or personal data, contact @nit_zunda_dev on X.
